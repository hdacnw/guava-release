## 🔁 Animated previews

The GIFs in [previews/](previews/) are generated from the corresponding local
MP4 files for inline display in the README. They cover the complete clips at the
same playback speed, sampled at 8 frames per second with 128 colors on a
360 × 256 canvas. The original aspect ratio is preserved with padding.

The overview video uses the framework figure as its preview. Full-resolution
MP4 videos remain available alongside the GIFs.
